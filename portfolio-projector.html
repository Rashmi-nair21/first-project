How this works. Each holding grows by a random monthly return drawn from its expected return and volatility. Holdings move together through a shared market factor, so a crash hits stocks at the same time, while bonds and gold move more independently. Contributions are split by your starting weights. Ending values are summarized as percentiles across all simulated paths.

Limits. The default assumptions are rough long-run estimates, not predictions, and real returns can fall outside the shaded range. Dividends are assumed reinvested. Taxes are ignored. This is an educational tool, not financial advice.
